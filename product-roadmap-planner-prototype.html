Stakeholder narrative

Q1 — Trust before growth. The roadmap opens with our most non-negotiable item: PCI-DSS payment tokenization. This is not a feature — it is a legal prerequisite. At $800K in risk avoidance and a 95% confidence score, it anchors Q1 unconditionally and clears the compliance gate for everything downstream. Alongside it, One-click checkout lands as our highest-conviction conversion driver — RICE score 872, backed by dual-source validation (customer feedback + sales) and a direct $2.1M ROI signal tied to the 15% conversion goal.

Q2 — Capture the intent we are creating. With a trusted, frictionless checkout live, Q2 activates retention. Abandoned cart recovery (RICE 756) is the highest-efficiency item in the backlog — $600K ROI at just 1 sprint of effort. It depends on checkout being live first: recovery emails lose effectiveness if the checkout experience is still friction-heavy. Real-time inventory sync ships in parallel, eliminating the top ops escalation — stockout complaints that cost $400K per year in support overhead.

Q3 — Platform stability at scale. Mobile app performance moves here deliberately. At 2.5 sprints it is a significant investment, but it follows inventory and infrastructure work that must be stable before we refactor the native layer. The $500K churn risk signal is real and this quarter has buffer for scope that will inevitably emerge.

Q4 — Strategic bet. AI product recommendations and Seller analytics close the year. Both are growth investments, not fixes. Placing them in Q4 is intentional: we need the platform to be reliable, compliant, and conversion-optimised before we layer intelligence on top. AI recommendations carry a $1.2M GMV upside and CEO sponsorship — Q4 gives us the stable foundation to execute it well.
